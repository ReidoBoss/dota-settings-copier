How it works

Takes about ten seconds.

  1. Close Dota 2Steam can stay open. Each account needs to have launched Dota once on this computer.
  2. Pick FROM and TOYour accounts are found and listed by name automatically.
  3. Click Copy SettingsEvery file is copied and checked. Then log in and play.
Steam Cloud tip: if Steam shows a "Cloud Sync Conflict" for Dota 2 after copying, choose the local files. Otherwise Steam brings back the old settings.
